    Regulators are signalling what they will want to see.

    In January 2026 the FDA and EMA published joint guiding principles for AI in drug development. They are non-binding. Our reading is that they lean towards documented data provenance, controlled and reproducible environments, and records of human review, though the wording is high level and open to interpretation.

    The approach page goes through the ten principles one by one and says how far Reprosia gets you on each, including where it does not help. For a concrete example, see the worked example, a real run of the tools with its output.

    Our reading draws on a third-party summary. Check the official text before you rely on any wording.

    Preparing R work for regulatory review?
