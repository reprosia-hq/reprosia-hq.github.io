    Four packages carry the evidence.

    They are open source, maintained by the same author, and usable without Reprosia.

    PackageWhat it doesStatus
    regulogTamper-evident, hash-chained audit logging designed around 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11. Ships with IQ/OQ/PQ qualification scripts.On CRAN
    lineagerRow-level data provenance and exclusion tracking, with provenance reports aligned to the CDISC Reviewer's Guide.On CRAN
    reproducrReproducibility auditing: session capture, dependency locking, script certification and drift detection.On CRAN
    baypriorBayesian prior elicitation for clinical trials, with conflict diagnostics, sensitivity analysis and regulatory reporting.On CRAN

    Regulators are asking for this kind of record.

    In January 2026 the FDA and EMA published joint guiding principles for AI in drug development. They are non-binding, and they point to documented data provenance, controlled and reproducible environments, and records of human review.

    The approach page goes through the ten principles one by one and says how far Reprosia gets you on each, including where it does not help.

    Our reading draws on a third-party summary. Check the official text before you rely on any wording.
