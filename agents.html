Agents prepare the paperwork. People decide.

Agents are in design, one per evidence package. They do the slow, structured work and every step goes into the same tamper-evident log. None of them is built yet.

One agent per package.

Each agent does the slow, structured work around one package. People review and sign off, and every step goes into the same tamper-evident log.
