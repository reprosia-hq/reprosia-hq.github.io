What I can help with.

Regulatory R implementation
Validation strategy, SOPs and system validation documentation for using R in GxP environments.
Reproducibility assessment
An independent review of an analysis pipeline: undocumented dependencies, environment risks and a prioritised remediation plan.
Package and environment validation
IQ/OQ/PQ documentation for R and R packages, ready for regulatory review.
Estimand specification
Support with ICH E9(R1) estimand strategy, from protocol definition through to implementation in the SAP.
SAS-to-R migration
Equivalence testing between SAS and R outputs, parallel validation documentation and a migration audit trail.
Training
Sessions for clinical statistics teams on R, reproducible workflows, estimands and Bayesian methods.
Qualification of AI-assisted workflows Planned
Evidence packs for teams adopting AI assistants in statistical programming. This follows the audited AI assist work and is not yet offered.

How an engagement runs.

  1. Conversation

    We talk through your environment, your quality system and what a reviewer would ask.

  2. Scoping

    A short written scope with deliverables, timing and what you need to provide.

  3. Delivery

    Documents and code you own, plus the open-source tools where they help.

  4. Follow-up

    A review after delivery to deal with questions from QA or auditors.
