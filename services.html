Consultancy for regulated statistical analysis.

Specialising in R and reproducible workflows, including moves from SAS. Reprosia takes on project work, advisory sessions and contract engagements for pharma, biotech and research teams.

What we help with.

Regulatory R implementation
Validation strategy, SOPs and system validation documentation for using R in GxP environments.
Package and environment validation
IQ/OQ/PQ documentation for R and R packages, using regulog's qualification scripts where they help.
Reproducibility and provenance audits
An independent review of an analysis pipeline: undocumented dependencies, environment risks, excluded records and a prioritised fix list.
SAS-to-R migration
Equivalence testing between SAS and R outputs, parallel validation documentation and a migration audit trail.
Statistical programming and analysis support
Analysis programming and code review (R specialism, SAS and Python workflows understood), and general statistical consultancy on study analysis, including Bayesian prior documentation with bayprior.
Shiny application development
R Shiny apps for analysis review, data exploration and internal tools, built with reproducibility in mind: version-controlled, tested, with the environment locked. Whether an app needs formal validation depends on how your team uses it, and that is agreed with your quality group. One public example: the live demo of the bayprior app, an open-source tool for documenting Bayesian priors.
AI-use governance
What to log when an AI assistant helps with an analysis, who reviews it and how to replay it. Starts with the free evidence kit.
Qualification of AI-assisted workflows Planned
Evidence packs for teams adopting AI assistants in statistical programming. This follows the audited AI assist work and is not yet offered.

Training.

Two topics, taught hands-on with your own kind of data and workflows. Half-day and full-day formats, for statisticians and statistical programmers.

Topic 1

R for clinical statistics teams

Getting productive in R for analysis and reporting, from data handling to tables, figures and reusable code.

Topic 2

Reproducible workflows

Scripted pipelines, version control, locked environments and documented runs, so results can be re-run and reviewed.

How an engagement runs.

  1. Conversation

    A short call about your environment, your quality system and what a reviewer would ask. We walk one real workflow with a one-page intake sheet, which you can also use on your own.

  2. Scoping

    A short written scope with deliverables, timing and what you need to provide.

  3. Delivery

    Documents and code you own, plus the open-source tools where they help.

  4. Follow-up

    A review after delivery to deal with questions from QA or auditors.
