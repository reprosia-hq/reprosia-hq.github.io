What the FDA and EMA AI principles ask for, and how far Reprosia gets you.

In January 2026 the two agencies published ten joint guiding principles for AI across the drug lifecycle. They are non-binding. This table turns them into things you could show a reviewer.

PrincipleWhat to be able to showWhat Reprosia gives you todaySupport
Human-centric by designWho reviewed, accepted, edited or rejected each AI output.regulog keeps a user-attributed log. Capturing review decisions on AI outputs is in development.Partial
Risk-based approachValidation effort that scales with the tool's context of use and risk.No software feature. Consulting can support the risk assessment.Guidance only
Adherence to standardsAI work sits inside existing GxP controls, audit trails included.regulog is designed around 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11. You decide which AI records are in scope and document it.Supported
Clear context of useThe task, scope and limits of each AI tool, written down.No software feature. Consulting and templates only.Guidance only
Multidisciplinary expertiseStatisticians, QA, IT and regulatory staff working together.Training and workshops.Guidance only
Data governance and documentationData sources, handling and lineage, tied to each run.lineager traces rows and exclusions from source to analysis dataset.Supported
Model design and developmentTraceable development, versioned releases and frozen environments.reproducr captures sessions and locks dependencies. It covers the R environment, not model training.Partial
Risk-based performance assessmentPredefined metrics and acceptance criteria, with error monitoring.reproducr flags drift when results change. Model-specific performance metrics are not covered.Partial
Lifecycle managementPlanned updates, re-validation and ongoing monitoring.Drift detection and replay help. The change-control process is yours.Partial
Clear, essential informationDocumentation of intended use, capabilities and limits.lineager generates structured provenance reports. AI-specific documentation templates are in development.Partial

Reading the table. Supported means an existing package produces the record. Partial means it covers part of the need, or the AI-specific part is still being built. Guidance only means help comes from consulting, not software.

Principle names and the evidence column paraphrase a third-party summary of the January 2026 principles, not the agencies' own text. Read the official documents before you cite wording, and decide with your QA group which AI records fall under 21 CFR Part 11.
